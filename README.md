# WorkWise AI – Workplace Productivity Assistant

## AI-Powered Workplace Productivity Assistant

WorkWise AI is a modern web application designed to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can support everyday workplace productivity.

The application combines AI productivity tools, prompt engineering principles and responsible AI practices in one workplace dashboard.

## Project Purpose

The purpose of WorkWise AI is to demonstrate practical applications of AI in workplace environments while promoting effective prompt engineering and responsible use of artificial intelligence.

## Core AI Productivity Tools

### 1. Smart Email Generator

The Smart Email Generator helps users create professional workplace emails.

Users can:

* Enter the purpose of an email
* Select a tone
* Generate an email
* Copy the generated email
* Reset the tool

Available tones include:

* Professional
* Friendly
* Formal
* Concise

### 2. Meeting Notes Summarizer

The Meeting Notes Summarizer helps convert meeting notes into a structured summary.

It identifies:

* Meeting summary
* Key discussion points
* Decisions
* Action items
* Responsible persons
* Deadlines

### 3. AI Task Planner

The AI Task Planner organises workplace goals and tasks.

It helps identify:

* Urgent tasks
* Important tasks
* Lower-priority tasks
* Recommended order
* Suggested deadlines

### 4. AI Research Assistant

The AI Research Assistant helps users structure workplace research.

It provides:

* Research overview
* Key points
* Suggested research questions
* Useful search terms
* Recommended presentation structure

Users are reminded to verify AI-generated information using reliable sources.

### 5. Workplace AI Chatbot

The chatbot provides general workplace productivity assistance.

It can assist with:

* Workplace communication
* Planning
* Productivity
* Writing
* Brainstorming
* Research
* General office tasks

## Prompt Engineering

WorkWise AI demonstrates the following prompt structure:

**Role + Task + Context + Requirements + Output Format**

Specific prompts can improve AI responses by reducing ambiguity and providing clearer instructions.

## Responsible AI

WorkWise AI promotes responsible AI practices.

Users are reminded to:

* Verify AI-generated information.
* Protect personal information.
* Never enter passwords.
* Never enter confidential company information.
* Check AI-generated content before using it professionally.
* Use AI to support human decision-making.
* Avoid discriminatory or unfair AI-assisted decisions.
