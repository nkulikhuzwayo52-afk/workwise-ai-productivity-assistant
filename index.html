PROMPT ENGINEERING

Write Better Prompts

Effective prompts give AI clear instructions, useful context, specific requirements and a desired output format.

Role Tell AI who it should act as.
Task Clearly explain what you want it to do.
Context Provide relevant background information.
Requirements Specify important conditions or limitations.
Output Format Explain how the response should be structured.
01 — EMAIL GENERATION

Act as a professional workplace communication assistant. Write an email to my manager requesting permission to attend a professional development workshop. The workshop takes place next Friday and will improve my workplace skills. Use a professional and respectful tone. Include a subject line, greeting, clear request, short explanation and professional closing.

02 — MEETING SUMMARISATION

Act as a professional meeting assistant. Summarise the meeting notes provided below. Identify the main discussion points, decisions, action items, responsible persons and deadlines when they are mentioned. Present the information using clear headings and bullet points.

03 — TASK PLANNING

Act as a workplace productivity coach. Organise the following list of tasks into a practical action plan. Identify urgent, important and lower-priority tasks. Recommend the best order for completing them and suggest realistic deadlines. Present the result in a clear table.

Why do specific prompts produce better results?

Specific prompts reduce ambiguity and give the AI clearer instructions. Providing a role, task, context, requirements and output format helps produce responses that are more relevant, consistent and useful.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Use AI Responsibly

AI can improve productivity, but human judgement and responsible use remain essential.

Human Verification

Always review and verify AI-generated information before using it professionally.

Accuracy

AI can produce inaccurate, incomplete or outdated information. Important facts should be checked.

Protect Information

Never enter passwords, confidential company information, financial information or sensitive personal information.

Privacy

Protect personal information and only provide information that is necessary for the task.

Human Judgement

AI should support human decision-making rather than replace human judgement.

Fairness

Do not use AI to make unfair or discriminatory decisions about people.
